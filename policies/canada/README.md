# Canada - AI Acceptable Use Policy set

**Status: PUBLISHED - verified within its stated scope: the federal Personal
Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act as consolidated to 21 June 2026,
and Quebec's private-sector Act as rewritten by Law 25, read with the privacy
commissioners' generative AI principles.** Every citation was read in the held
instruments. Alberta and British Columbia, the regulations under both Acts, human
rights and consumer law are named as not held. Bill C-36 is named as pending and
relied on for nothing.
**Educational, not legal advice** - review by qualified counsel before adoption.

## Purpose

An acceptable use policy for AI tools in an organisation subject to Canadian law, with
an enterprise governance annex. Obligations are stated as rules a team can apply;
provision identifiers live in the research note, not the policy body.

## What shapes the Canadian edition

1. **There is no Canadian AI Act, and the federal privacy reform is a bill.** The
   artificial intelligence bill died with Bill C-27 in January 2025. Bill C-36, the
   third attempt at federal reform, had first reading on 15 June 2026. The set states
   what it would add - a definition of automated decision systems that reaches
   assistance as well as replacement, a public account of their use, an explanation on
   request, written representations to a reviewer - and relies on none of it.
2. **Two regimes apply, and the stricter one is provincial.** PIPEDA governs commercial
   activity federally and employee information only in federal works. Quebec's Act, as
   amended between 2022 and 2024, governs every enterprise there with a mandatory
   officer, a privacy impact assessment for every new system, express consent for
   sensitive information, an assessment before information leaves Quebec, and
   administrative penalties up to the greater of $10 million or 2% of worldwide
   turnover. The set applies the stricter rule everywhere as a house standard.
3. **The only enacted automated-decision duty in Canada is Quebec's, and it turns on
   one word.** Since 22 September 2023, a decision based **exclusively** on automated
   processing carries a duty to inform, to explain on request (information used,
   reasons, principal factors and parameters, right to correction), and to hear
   observations from the person through a member of staff able to review. Failing to
   inform or to hear is a penalty ground. A decision a person meaningfully makes with AI
   support is outside the duty - so the set makes that a recorded determination for
   every system, and applies the same three rights to substantially automated
   decisions as a house standard.
4. **Inferring is collecting, and public does not mean free.** The commissioners have
   said that an inference about an identifiable person is a collection requiring legal
   authority, and that personal information accessible online remains regulated. The
   federal publicly-available exception reaches only what regulations specify.

## The consolidation gap

Légis Québec, the official consolidation site, was unavailable at build. The set was
built from the assented amending Act, which sets out every provision relied on in full,
and records the consolidation as an open item. The commencement schedule was read from
the amending Act's own coming-into-force section.

## Open gates (stated, not hidden)

1. **The consolidated Quebec Act is not held** - to be added for its "updated to" date
   and any amendment since 2021 to the relied-on sections.
2. **The regulations under both Acts are not held** - breach records, incident notice
   content, publicly available information. The set says a regulation governs and does
   not say what it contains.
3. **Alberta and British Columbia are not held** - substantially similar provincial
   Acts that displace PIPEDA within the province.
4. **The set cannot be run through the Data Privacy platform's verification pipeline**
   because Canada is not one of the platform's seven jurisdictions. Verification is by
   direct reading, recorded in the source register.
