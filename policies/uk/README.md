# AI Acceptable Use Policy set - United Kingdom

**AIGP domain:** Domain 5 - Implementing and operating AI governance
**Status:** Published. Verified against consolidated primary legislation, 27 August 2026.
**Educational, not legal advice** - review by your DPO or legal counsel before adoption.

## What this is

A complete, usable AI governance policy set for a UK organisation, current to
the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025:

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [UK_AI_Acceptable_Use_Policy.md](UK_AI_Acceptable_Use_Policy.md) | The full policy (v1.0) - approved tools, levels of use, personal data, verification of output, human oversight, incidents and complaints |
| [UK_AI_Use_OnePager.md](UK_AI_Use_OnePager.md) | The one-page staff guide - the rules people actually follow day to day |
| [UK_AI_Governance_Enterprise_Annex.md](UK_AI_Governance_Enterprise_Annex.md) | For large organisations - governance committee, AI system inventory, lifecycle controls, supplier terms, exceptions, agentic AI, assurance |
| [UK_policy_research_note.md](UK_policy_research_note.md) | The working: every obligation mapped to its source and article, verification dates, house standards that deliberately exceed statute, watch items |

Word versions are in [downloads/](downloads/).

## Primary sources used

The UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 **as amended** (revised consolidated
texts), the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 and its commencement regulations,
the Equality Act 2010, the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, and current
ICO guidance. Full citations with verification dates are in the research note.

## Design choices worth knowing

- The policy requires meaningful human review **before** significant automated
  decisions take effect - deliberately stricter than the post-DUAA statutory
  floor. The research note records why.
- Article numbers are kept out of the policy body and in the research note, so
  the policy reads as rules, not as a statute concordance.
