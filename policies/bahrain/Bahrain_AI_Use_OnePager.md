# Using AI at Work - What You Need to Know (Bahrain)

**[ORGANISATION] - one-page guide.** The full AI Acceptable Use Policy sits behind
this; this is what applies to your day-to-day. Some of these rules carry
criminal penalties in Bahrain - when in doubt, ask [POLICY OWNER] first.

---

## Do

- **Use only approved AI tools** (see the approved list) on your work account.
- **Check everything AI produces.** Treat it as a draft - verify facts, figures,
  names, quotes, and code before you rely on it or send it out. You are
  accountable for what you use.
- **Keep it non-sensitive** unless the tool and the data are both approved for it.
- **Ask first** if a tool you need isn't on the list - requests are quick.

## Don't

- **Don't paste in sensitive data:** personal data outside an approved tool,
  health or biometric or genetic data, data about criminal proceedings, payment
  details, passwords, secrets, or confidential code.
- **Don't use personal AI accounts** for work.
- **Don't let AI make the final call** on decisions about people (hiring, pay,
  eligibility) - a person must review and be able to override it.
- **Don't pass AI output off as checked** when it isn't.
- **Don't create fake images/audio/video of real people** without authorisation.
- **Don't start or pilot AI that watches or identifies people** - camera
  analytics, facial or voice recognition, matching people across databases.
  These need the regulator's written permission BEFORE they begin (no reply
  counts as no), and starting without it is a criminal offence. Ask first.

## Before any new AI use goes live

Some AI processing must be notified to the regulator before it begins, and some
may not begin until written permission is received - decided per system, before
go-live. New AI tool or new use? Check with [DATA PROTECTION LEAD] first.

## If you're using AI with personal data

There must be a lawful ground before processing starts - where it is consent,
it must be explicit and in writing (electronic counts). Use only the minimum
data; high-risk uses need an assessment first. Check before you start.

## Where the AI runs matters

Personal data must not leave Bahrain unless the destination is on the approved
country list or the regulator has permitted the transfer. Many major hosting
regions are listed - but where a cloud AI tool actually processes data must be
established and recorded before approval. Don't assume.

## If AI talks to customers

Tell them it's AI, let them reach a human, and hold it to our accuracy standards.

## If something goes wrong

Told AI something you shouldn't have? Acted on wrong or unfair output?
**Report it straight away** to [POLICY OWNER / DATA PROTECTION LEAD] - the
regulator-notification clock runs from discovery, so report first, investigate
second. Good-faith reporting is the right thing to do - not a disciplinary trap.
