## 6. Sources NOT used, as a matter of method

- **No web content entered any classification rule.** Both instruments were
  supplied as files and read directly; no web page was consulted for any
  provision, date, or annex item.
- **No secondary commentary, law-firm briefing, or comparative summary.** The
  commencement dates - which secondary sources still frequently get wrong - were
  taken from Article 113 of the consolidated text, and the Annex III list from
  the Annex itself, item by item.
- **No AI-generated legal content.** Every provision cited in the research note
  resolves to text in a file in section 1.

## 7. The verification that mattered most

**That the Digital Omnibus left Annex III alone.** The classification changes in
Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 sit in Article 6(1a)-(1c) and Annex I - the product
route - and in the two new prohibitions. It would be easy to assume a
"simplification" regulation trimmed the high-risk list; it did not. Every item
in Annex III carries the base-text marker in the consolidation, and the omnibus
contains no amendment to it. The whole eight-area list, verified item by item,
stands as adopted in 2024 - while the dates on which it bites moved by more
than a year. Both halves of that sentence come from the primary text, and
neither could safely have come from anywhere else.
