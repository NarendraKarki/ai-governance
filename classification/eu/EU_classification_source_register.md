## 4. Method

- Annex III was extracted from the consolidated PDF and read sub-point by
  sub-point. The extraction was checked for amendment markers (none in Annex III;
  markers present in Annex I) and the Omnibus Art 1 amendment list was read to
  confirm that no Annex III amendment exists.
- Every date in the artifact was taken from Art 113 as amended and Art 111 as
  amended, in the consolidated text, and cross-checked against Art 1 items (39)
  and (40) of the Omnibus.
- Article 6(1a)-(1c) and the amended Art 3(14) were read in both the consolidation
  and the Omnibus to confirm the wording of the safety-component narrowing.
- **No web content entered any rule.** The overview page was used to identify that
  guidance documents exist, so that their absence could be recorded here.
- **No secondary commentary** was used.

## 6. The verification that mattered most

Two things, this time.

**First, that Annex III did not change.** The Omnibus changed a great deal around
the high-risk regime - the safety-component definition, the Annex I lists, the
dates, the derogation's registration content - and it is easy to assume it touched
the list itself. It did not. The consolidation shows no amendment marker in Annex
III and the Omnibus's own amendment list contains none. Every item in the
walkthrough is therefore the 2024 text, read in the 2026 consolidation.

**Second, that Art 6(5) is carved out of the deferral.** The deferral of Chapter
III Sections 1-3 to December 2027 and August 2028 is stated in Art 113(c) "with the
exception of Article 6(5)". The Commission's classification guidelines were never
deferred. That is the textual reason classification is a present-tense task while
the obligations that follow from it are not.
